FLUXO DE EVENTOS - ABRIR MODAL:
===============================

Usuario → Click Button
     ↓
onclick="window.openProcessModal(data)" ou similar
     ↓
event.stopPropagation()  [Previne propagação para overlay]
     ↓
window.closeAllModals()  [Fecha outras modais abertas]
     ↓
document.getElementById('id').classList.add('visible')
     ↓
document.getElementById('id').style.display = 'flex'
     ↓
overlay.classList.add('visible')
     ↓
Modal Appears + Overlay Fades In (CSS Transition: 0.2s)
     ↓
Focus → Primeiro elemento interativo
     ↓
Modal Pronta para Interação ✓


FLUXO DE EVENTOS - FECHAR MODAL:
================================

Usuario → Click X Button / ESC Key / Clique Backdrop
     ↓
Event Listener Acionado
     ↓
event.stopPropagation()  [Previne propagação]
     ↓
window.closeModal('id') ou window.ConfirmModal.cancel()
     ↓
modal.classList.remove('visible')
     ↓
modal.style.display = 'none'
     ↓
overlay.classList.remove('visible')
     ↓
Modal Disappears (CSS Transition)
     ↓
Focus Volta / Menu Fecha
     ↓
Modal Fechada ✓


FLUXO DE EVENTOS - ABRIR MENU:
==============================

Usuario → Click Menu Icon (#menu-toggle)
     ↓
addEventListener('click', function(e) { ... })
     ↓
e.stopPropagation()
     ↓
window.toggleMenu()
     ↓
sideMenu.classList.toggle('open')
     ↓
overlay.classList.toggle('visible')
     ↓
Menu Slides In / Overlay Appears
     ↓
Menu Pronto ✓


FLUXO DE EVENTOS - FECHAR MENU:
===============================

Usuario → Click X / Click Item / Click Overlay / ESC
     ↓
Event Listener Acionado
     ↓
window.toggleMenu() ou window.closeAllModals()
     ↓
sideMenu.classList.remove('open')
     ↓
overlay.classList.remove('visible')
     ↓
submenu.forEach(sub => sub.classList.remove('open'))
     ↓
Menu Slides Out / Overlay Disappears
     ↓
Menu Fechado ✓
